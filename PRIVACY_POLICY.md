# Privacy Policy for PartX - File Splitter & Joiner

**Last updated:** October 12, 2026

PartX - File Splitter & Joiner ("PartX", "the Application", "we", "our", or "us") is an open-source desktop software developed by Dhruv Gupta. We respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring transparency about how your data is handled.

---

## 1. No Personal Data Collected
PartX does not collect, record, transmit, sell, or store any personal information, telemetry, tracking data, device identifiers, or usage analytics. 

You can use PartX without registering an account, providing contact details, or sharing any personal identity data.

---

## 2. Local File Processing Only
- All operations (splitting files, recombining chunks, calculating and verifying SHA-256 / SFV checksums) occur **entirely locally on your device**.
- No file contents, file names, file metadata, or file directories are ever uploaded to external servers, cloud services, or third parties.
- The Application only accesses files and directories that you explicitly select or drag-and-drop into the interface.

---

## 3. Network and Internet Connectivity
PartX operates completely offline and requires no active internet connection to execute any of its core capabilities:
- No background network requests are initiated.
- No analytics or third-party advertising SDKs are embedded.
- External links (such as navigating to the GitHub repository) open in your default system browser only when explicitly clicked by you.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services
PartX does not integrate with any third-party tracking, profiling, advertising, or cloud storage APIs.

---

## 5. Security of Your Files
Because PartX does not store or transmit your data to remote servers, the security of your files is maintained under your local machine's operating system permissions and security policies.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy
The Application does not collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
